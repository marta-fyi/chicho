Sort the food: the place sheets and undo

Tapping a place with nothing selected opens its list: a bottom sheet with a scrim, Close and a swipe down, the place's name and meaning, a 44px row per food with Move (Keep out · Not a fan · Tried already · Untouched · Back to the pile); the family's own items have Remove; Keep out adds "Also skipped (they contain {allergen})". Every move shows the undo line and closes the row; Undo reverses only the latest change, for every kind of change; the undo line goes after 5 s. Every line should be PASS.
