Sort the food: tap, select, send

A tap (released within 10px and 500 ms) toggles a shape's selection: a 3px ink ring at a 4px offset; the status row says how many, with Clear. A shape still moving faster than 20 px/s ignores taps; so does a long press. Tapping a place sends the whole selection there (the same drop, 40 ms apart); "Select all" selects the pile and becomes "Clear". Every line should be PASS.
