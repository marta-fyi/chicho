Sort the food: drag to sort

Touch pointers, dispatched as a finger would: a press that moves past 10px lifts the shape (top layer, a --bg ring, no scale), it follows 1:1 keeping the touch offset and pushes what it meets; over a place (or within 48px of one) that place swells to 1.03 with a 6% wash and the other three dim to 50%; let go there and the food is sorted: the shape shrinks and fades into the cell, one ring ripples, the name appears, the progress moves, the undo line shows and the pile re-flows. Let go elsewhere and it stays where it stopped. Every line should be PASS.
