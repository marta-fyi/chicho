# Decisions

Where the brief's rules met each other, the principles won. Every number marked **verify** is a planning aid, not advice, and needs checking against the named kind of source before anyone relies on it.

## Sort the food (25 Oct 2026, supersedes "Bubbles that pop" for the Allergens step)

Marta's brief: the parent drags the foods into four places (Keep out, Not a fan, Tried already, Untouched), and the plan reads one selector, `getFoodRules(profile, catalog)` (`sortcore.js`). Every number is in `sortconfig.js`.

- **The manifest carries the sort's layer** (`FOOD_SORT` in `foods.js`): `contains` (the big ones in each food, possibly none) and `minMonths` (the generator's own minimum; a test fails if they drift apart). There is no default: a food missing from `FOOD_SORT` has no `contains`, and `tests/foodsort.html` fails.
- **The big ones are foods of their own**: peanut, tree nuts, wheat, soy, fish, shellfish and sesame join egg and milk in the manifest, with no drawing (the sort is text only). White fish gave the bare alias "fish" to the allergen; milk lost "breast milk" (it isn't the allergen) and gained "dairy" and "cow's milk".
- **One set per food**: never beats avoid, avoid beats the food's own place, and the food's own place beats what its allergens say (milk Tried, cheese Kept out: cheese is never). A food is free through its allergens only when all of them were tried (meatballs need egg and wheat). An unsorted big one is untouched.
- **Not a fan on an allergen** avoids every food that contains it (they're used only when a slot has nothing else); a typed "Keep out" that matches a big one ("peanuts") keeps out everything containing it.
- **The generator reads `getFoodRules` and nothing else from the sort** (`foodRules` in index.html keeps it as sets, worked out once per change). `isAllowed` drops `neverIds` (and, under 6 months, any food carrying an untouched allergen); the dealer uses a Not a fan food only when its slot has no other food (it was a 0.3 weight before), and so do the iron swap, the allergen swap and the near-family staples; a meal takes at most one new food (Untouched, and not offered yet), the dealer leaving the slot empty rather than adding a second (a meal the allergen spacing emptied gets its groups back from foods with no first allergen, `fillMeals`); and the allergen spacing counts every big one not left Untouched as met. Shuffle keeps the same one-new-food rule.
- **The one-new-food rule now holds in a first week too.** Before, nothing counted as new until something had been offered. Now an unsorted everyday food is Untouched, so a family that skips the everyday pile gets meals of one food until foods are offered (or sorted into Tried already). That is what the brief's rule says; worth a look in real use.
- **Profiles from before the sort** read through `legacyFoodSort` (allergens avoid → Keep out, ok → Tried already; their own keep-outs and not-a-fans → custom entries matched to the manifest; the Meat and Gluten diets → custom Keep outs of the foods their words name; "Tried, all fine" → Tried already; an onboarded family's unanswered big ones → Untouched). Nothing is written until the sort is opened, so a reload never rewrites a profile.
- **The old fields are a mirror** once there's a sort (`syncLegacyFromSort`): Profile's Off the plate, Home's allergen firsts and the snapshot link still read `allergens` and `avoid`. Profile's "Tried, all fine" writes the sort (`setBigPlace`).
- **Shapes** (`makeShape`, `sortcore.js`): eight radii from 38–62%, drawn again while the shortest is under 0.7 of the longest (or all are 50%); `d` is the shorter side and the aspect (0.85–1.2) stretches the other, so the smallest inscribed circle stays near `d` (measured from the CSS outline itself, radii scaled as CSS does when a side's two add up to more than 100%). Each food's shape comes from the seed and its own id, so a food keeps its shape when the pile around it changes.
- **Labels** fit 76% of the width at 11–14px in at most two balanced lines. A single long word ("Strawberries") can't fit an 84px shape at 11px, so a shape is never narrower than its label needs at 11px (the one exception to MAX_D, by up to ~20px).
- **Packing** works on the rotated box for the edges and on the 0.82 collision circles between shapes. When a field can't hold the pile at MIN_D, it shrinks to MIN_D, stops, and the result says so (`ok:false`); `tools/seeds.mjs` reports it.
- **Seeds**: `tools/seeds.mjs` scores 200 seeds per phone size (packing, smallest inscribed circle, the evenest radii, a balanced pile) and keeps 12 in `curatedSeeds.json`. It scores against the fields the screen really lays out (`docs/sortfields.json`, measured by `tools/devices.mjs`); before the first measurement it used the layout budget's minimum piles, where 375×667's 170px can't hold the nine big ones at MIN_D (0 of 200 pass).
- **Where it lives**: the onboarding's Allergens step (`onbKeepout` mounts `mountSortFood`), and Profile's Off the plate "Edit" opens the same screen with Save and Close. The bubble pile stays for the Kitchen step only. The top bar (Back, the six segments, Close) is the onboarding's, as the brief asked.
- **The sort is written down the first time the screen opens** (from the old fields, through `legacyFoodSort`), and saved on every change.
- **The title is two lines** at 375–430px wide: "Sort the food for Lula." is 23 characters, and mono at 26px fits about 15 on a line beside the progress. The layout budget's "title to one line" isn't reachable at 26px; the fallbacks (below) take the space back instead.
- **The status row's gate** ("Sort the big ones first · {n} to go") shows once there's been a sort, or when Next is tapped while it waits: before the first sort the hint ("Drag Egg to where it belongs.") is the row's only line, because the gate outranks the hints and would otherwise hide it for good.
- **The engine** (`sortStep`, `sortcore.js`): circles of 0.82 of each shape's radius, mass by area, a fixed 1/120 s step. A held shape is kinematic (it follows the finger and doesn't give); a contact passes on velocity along the normal (restitution 0.3) and, through friction at the contact point (`FRICTION = 0.5`, a new constant), spin: that's what turns an off-centre push into a tumble. With only the half-lives, a 400 px/s push takes ~0.8 s to reach 2 px/s, past the brief's ~0.4 s; so under `REST_SPEED` (40 px/s) and `REST_SPIN` (0.4 rad/s) each step takes off another 15% (`REST_DAMP`), and a push settles in ~0.5 s. Settled means under the sleep thresholds; the loop then sleeps after another 500 ms of stillness, as the brief says.
- **The tuning panel** lives in `tools/sorttune.js`, outside the service worker's shell, and loads only for `?tune=1` on localhost: there is no build step to exclude it from, so production never fetches it.
- **Drag to sort**: the place under the finger wins, else the nearest within 48px (measured from the cell's edge). The held shape is moved to the end of the field (the top layer) and drawn with the `--bg` ring; it can leave the field to reach the places. "Its label sharpens" is read as a little more tracking on the target's label (it's already full ink and 500); the swell is a 160 ms transform. A shape coming back (Undo, Back to the pile) rises in the field's lower middle and the re-flow finds it a place, so nothing else jumps; a re-flow draws each grown shape at its new size, scaled from the old, so the label grows with it and lands at its fitted size.
- **A place's list** stays open after a move (the row leaves it, the undo line shows behind the scrim, and Undo repaints the list). Move offers the other three places and "Back to the pile"; the family's own items have only Remove ("Kiwi removed · Undo"). Keep out's "Also skipped (they contain {allergen})" comes once per kept-out allergen, typed ones included ("peanuts"), followed by the traces line. A swipe down closes the sheet from its top (past 90px, or a quick flick past 30px).
- **Undo** holds one change, whatever it was: a drop, a send, a Move, Back to the pile, a Remove, an Add.
- **Something else** keeps its text as typed ("Épinards"), matched or not; the same text again (by its normalised form) changes the existing item's place rather than adding a second. At twelve, Add says "That's twelve of your own. Remove one to add another." The result line lists everything the match reaches ("peanuts" → "Also skips peanut and peanut butter.").
- **The first pile done** pulses the onboarding's glow plane (opacity and scale, 900 ms; the dark theme has no glow, so nothing shows there), and the summary holds the status row for 4 s, over the undo line of the last sort (Undo is still there for the second it has left afterwards). The everyday pile opens on the first tab that has foods. Sending a big one back to the pile brings "The big ones" back, with the gate; placing it again returns to the everyday foods at once, with no second summary or pulse.
- **"Something else" sits in every everyday tab**, as the brief says, but not in "The big ones": so under 6 months, where there's no second pile, a family can't add their own yet. Worth deciding.
- **The everyday pile counts "Something else" in its twelve** (eleven foods and it). A food whose plain name would read as a locked one shows how the plan serves it: "Grapes" is "Grapes, quartered".
- **The wiggle** (±6°, 500 ms) goes to the first shape of a pile left untouched for 1.2 s, each time a pile arrives. **The fall at Next** drops the remaining shapes from the lowest up, over about 500 ms; onContinue() comes when the last has gone, once.
- **Matching what's typed**: trimmed, lower-cased, accents and apostrophes dropped, then simple plurals word by word (berries → berry, peaches → peach, tomatoes → tomato, grapes → grape; hummus and couscous keep their s), against every food's label, id and aliases; and a name that holds the typed words as a whole word counts too ("beef" is the meatballs as well, "cheese" is cottage cheese, "potato" is sweet potato), so a kept-out word never lets a food through under a longer name.

## Meal times in onboarding (24 Oct 2026, supersedes the "late question" status of meal times)

Draw → Spell → Birthday → Allergens → Meal times → Reveal → Done → Home; under 6 months, Allergens → Closing → Home. Meal times are a day axis, one component (`mealtimes.js`) with two hosts: the onboarding's step 5, and Profile > Settings > Meal times (Save, Close).

**The day-axis addendum isn't on disk.** This addendum builds on "Meal times on a day axis", which never shipped and whose text I don't have; the old screen was a list of `<input type="time">` rows. Marta chose to have it inferred from this addendum's hints, so every one of the following is my reading, not a spec:

- **The axis.** 6:00 to 21:00 down a vertical hairline (`MT` in `mealtimes.js`), a tick every hour, the reader's clock every three hours on the left ("6 AM", "9 AM"…). Each meal is a marker: a short ink tick across the hairline, its name and time ("Lunch 12:30 PM") on the right. The gap between two meals is a small label at their midpoint, right-aligned ("3 h 30"). A time outside 6:00–21:00 can't be set; a family whose breakfast is at 5:30 has to pick 6:00 **(a guess; the range is one constant)**.
- **Drag.** The finger takes the nearest marker within 44px (by height alone: one column, so at 60 minutes apart each marker owns about 22px of the axis at 375×667). It follows 1:1, snaps live to 15 minutes, and pushes its neighbours to keep **an hour between meals** (the minimum spacing the addendum's iPhone check implies). Markers never pass each other, so the order of the day is the order of the meals. Each drag resolves from where it started (`mtResolve` is pure), so a neighbour comes back while the finger does; on the drop it's kept where it is. Past either end of the day (or of the room its neighbours leave it) a marker gives like a rubber band, 30% of the finger, easing toward 40px, and springs back on release.
- **Tap and type.** A tap selects a marker; the strip under the sentence then says "Lunch around 12:30 PM" with "Type a time" (and "Remove" on a snack). The typed field sits in the strip, above the axis, so the keyboard can't cover it. **The keyboard rule:** the keyboard never opens by itself (arrival focuses the sentence); it opens only from "Type a time", inside the tap as iOS needs; its keyboard is `text` in a 12-hour locale (for "7pm") and `decimal` in a 24-hour one; and the axis holds still while it's up (it doesn't re-lay itself out for the shorter screen). `parseTyped` reads "7:30", "7.30", "730", "19h30", "7pm", "7:30 p.m.", "12a"; a bare hour from 1 to 12 is the one nearer where the meal is now ("7" for dinner is 19:00). A time it can't read says "Try 12:30 or 7pm."; outside the day, "Between 6:00 AM and 9:00 PM."; a time that would cross a neighbour is held an hour from it and says "Moved to {time}, to keep an hour between meals."
- **Snacks.** Each stage's config now lists the snacks it allows (`snacks` in `STAGES`): none for first tastes, one afternoon snack (off by default) for 9–11 months, both for 12 months up. "Add a snack" shows while one is free and there are two hours of room for it; it lands at its usual time if that keeps an hour from both neighbours, otherwise halfway. A selected snack has "Remove". The choice is kept as `profile.mealSlots = {base, snacks}`, where `base` is the stage's slot list it was made against.
- **The sun.** Light theme only (the dark theme keeps the night sky). The glow moves to the selected (or held) marker and takes the colour of its hour, blended between stops (`SUN` in `theme.js`: 6:00 peach, 10:00 the grain yellow, 14:00 a warmer yellow, 17:00 apricot, 19:00 rose, 21:00 lilac), all near the food groups' lightness; `tests/contrast.html` holds every stop to the same AA zones as the five groups. Untouched, it waits by lunch in 10:00's colour. While a marker is held it repaints instantly (only when the snapped time changes); otherwise it glides 300 ms. Leaving the screen, it crossfades to the Reveal's cycle over 1.2 s and moves back to its place.
- **Text only.** No icons, cards or hover; `tools/check.mjs` now fails on any emoji or pictograph in the copy or the component, and on icon markup in the component (its one SVG is the drawn name). The food data's `emoji`/`icon` fields are stored but never shown, so the lint leaves them alone.

**This addendum, as written, and where I read it:**

## Kitchen redesign (23 Oct 2026, supersedes every earlier Kitchen rule)

Kitchen answers one question at a time: **Buy**, **At home**, **Prep**, behind a text switch. The four old sections (To buy, Running low, At home, Freezer and prep) are gone, and so is every Kitchen detail in the swipe-navigation section below. The code lives in `kitchen.js`; its numbers are one frozen object, `KITCHEN`.

**The header and the switch**

- The header is pinned over the scroll area, which starts under it: its `padding-top` and `scroll-padding-top` are set to the header's measured height (a `ResizeObserver`), so nothing is clipped behind it at rest. Its surface is `--bg` at 88% (`--head-bg`, one token per theme, since the colour lint allows no colour outside the tokens) with `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)`. **The surface fades in with its hairline (150 ms)** once anything scrolls under it. At rest only the Kitchen's glow sits behind the title, and a solid surface would have hidden it.
- The header zone for vertical navigation is the whole pinned header, measured, including the view switch (it was a fixed 120px).
- The last view is remembered in `sessionStorage`, so it lasts for the tab's session. The arrow keys switch views on the Kitchen, as they switch screens elsewhere.

**Buy**

- **Ticking folds the row away with transforms only.** The ring fills (180 ms), then the row scales to nothing while everything below it slides up by its height (200 ms). ## Patterns (one thumb, messy hands, quiet)

- **Change, one at a time.** "Change" on a food shows one safe alternative (the same gate as the generator, drawn foods first, a stable order per food); "Another" crossfades to the next, "Keep" takes it, with the usual 5-second Undo.
- **Swipe a row.** Right marks the meal offered (its undo line follows), left opens it in place on Change for its main food. The ring and the tap stay the visible path. A swipe that starts within 24px of either edge is left to Safari's back swipe; a drag that moves 10px vertically first is the page's scroll (`touch-action: pan-y`); under 72px it springs back.
- **How it went, in the same motion.** Ticking a meal (pill, ring or swipe) brings "Loved it · Ate some · Not today" up inline under it, for the open meal and for rows alike. It fades away on its own after 10 s if ignored, and 1.6 s after an answer; nothing waits for it. (Before, the row waited for the 5-second undo and then stayed.) The reward stays what it was: the ring's check draws in (~180 ms) and the glow pulses once.
- **Scrub through the day.** Swiping the headline area sideways steps through that day's meals (left: later, right: earlier, stopping at the ends). The headline ("{Meal} around {time}."), the open meal and the glow crossfade to it; the moment line becomes a quiet "Back to now". The next meal boundary returns to now on its own.
- **Tomorrow mode.** The top-right date is now a button: a tap (or a swipe left) re-skins the page to tomorrow's plan: the corner shows tomorrow's date, the headline is tomorrow's first meal, the rest of tomorrow is listed under it, nothing from today is, and the moment line reads "Tomorrow · Back to today". Scrubbing works inside it. "Keep screen on" hides there (cooking happens today).
- **Press and hold for something big.** "Start a new week" (in This week) fills a ring over one second while held (pointer, or Space/Enter held); letting go early springs it back and nothing happens. It then sets the new week straight away, with "New week set. Undo" for 5 seconds that restores the old plan exactly. The "Love it" reveal stays for the first week and the "Set the week" moment line.
- **Cook mode.** "Start cooking" (today's open meal) turns the home into one step at a time: each cooked food's one-line how, longest-cooking first, then "That's it. Serve it all as …". The step is the headline (large type on the glow, which dims to 55%), "Step n of N" and its timer sit on the moment line, Next is an 88px button fixed in the thumb zone, and a sideways swipe on the step moves too. It's a mode of the page, not an overlay (the sheet stays the only overlay). The screen stays awake while cooking (Wake Lock, released after unless it was already on); Escape or "Stop cooking" leaves.
- **Timers, honestly.** A step with minutes offers a one-tap timer: an end time in `state.timers` (it survives a reload; one 6 hours gone is forgotten), a thin ring whose arc is set once a second (never animated), the time left in the tab title ("Sweet potato · 3:12"), and "Time's up: …" when Chicho is looked at again. The page says plainly that it can't ring when the phone is locked or in another app; reliable alerts would need web push through the installed app and a small backend (out of scope, like reminders).
- **Portions.** In cook mode, 1 · 2 · 4 rescales every amount ("35 g" × 2 = "70 g"; words stay as they are), which pairs with the "cook double and freeze half" line.
- **Quick add.** "Add something else" is one field; from two letters, up to four suggestions follow what's typed (names and aliases that start with it first, drawn foods first), each with its bullet. Only foods that pass the generator's gate and no allergy are ever suggested. One tap (or Enter, for the first) adds it with the stage's amount and texture, logged as offered, with "{Food} added. Undo" for 5 seconds. A typed food that isn't in the pool can still be added by Enter, as before.
- **In the store.** The Shop sheet has an "In the store" toggle: the list regroups by food group (Variety's order, "Everything else" last, extras included), the sheet goes to its large detent and the screen stays awake until the sheet closes. A thin ring ticks each thing; bought things move to "In stock", which is folded away (a count) until tapped. Nothing turns red when something runs out: "runs out Friday" is words, in the ink.
- **The staples shortcut.** The first time the shopping list is opened (that day only), it starts with one sentence, "Already in the kitchen…", and the staples (the onboarding's kitchen list) that are on this list as pills. A tap puts one in stock at its usual pack size, so it comes off the list (a second tap puts it back); "That's all" puts the question away for good. It's the onboarding's late "kitchen" question, asked where it pays off.
- **Undo instead of confirm** now covers changing a food, adding a food, offering a meal and starting a new week (5 seconds each); outcomes, bought rings and staples undo by a second tap.
- **The glow's width.** The glow's wrapper is sized from the page's measured width (`--vw`), not `100vw`, which includes a classic scrollbar and overflowed by its width wherever scrollbars aren't overlays.

## How I read the ambiguous parts

- **"Day finished" vs "after the last meal".** "That's today done." shows when every meal today was offered (or skipped). If the day ends with meals not offered, the headline is "{Meal} tomorrow around {time}." instead, so it never implies something was missed.
- **Dusk.** The sky has four phases (dawn, day, golden, evening). Only *evening* (19:30–05:00, the navy tint) is night/dusk: ink turns white there. Golden hour keeps black ink (measured 7.6:1 or better).
- **"Stage changed this month."** The stage line shows while the child is in the first month of a new stage and the week hasn't been updated; "Later" hides it until tomorrow.
- **Shopping day.** There is no shopping day unless one is set (`profile.shopDay`, a weekday number); the shop line otherwise shows when something runs out within 24 hours, before 19:00.
- **A meal offered from its own pill stays open** (Offered · Undo, then the outcome row) until the next meal boundary, so the Undo and the outcomes are where the tap was.
- **6 months starts with lunch only; breakfast joins at 7 months.** (verify, see below)
- **Allergen safety vs preference.** Each food's record keeps `safety` (notyet / introduced, with the date / no reaction noted on a date / keepout) and `preference` (the last outcome) separately; an outcome never changes safety.
- **"No banned strings in source."** The check covers `index.html`, `home.js`, `foods.js`, `content.js` and `sw.js`. The schema-8 migration must read the old field names to convert old saves; those two lines carry a `legacy:` marker and are the only exception. Tests are not scanned (they assert the words are absent).
- **Methods.** The cooking layer adds `toast` and `none` (stirred in) to steam / roast / boil / raw, so every food has an honest one-line how. Only steam / roast / boil foods count as cook-ahead.
- **Legumes are canned** unless a pantry item is marked dried (`stock[key].dried`), so "soak" only appears then.
- **Delivery granularity.** The v2 delivery list asked for nine commits. The pieces share one module (`home.js`) and one test build, so they landed together (each step's tests in the same commit) rather than as nine commits that each pass on their own.

## Sharing and the link

- **SMS links.** iOS and macOS take `sms:{number}&body={text}`; Android and everything else `sms:{number}?body={text}` (`platform()` reads the user agent; iPadOS reports itself as a Mac, which takes the Apple form). If the page is still visible ~1.4 s after opening the link, it falls back to the share sheet, then to copying the text with a short "Copied". Nothing claims more than "opens Messages".
- **Numbers** are normalised to digits and a leading `+`, stored only in localStorage, and never put into a link or a payload (tested).
- **The caretaker link** is `{origin}{path}#/s/{payload}`: the payload is in the fragment, which browsers never send to a server. `v: 1`; gzip (CompressionStream) + base64url, or a plain base64url fallback (`z…` / `j…`). It holds the name, age in months (not the birth date), allergies, dislikes, locked items and the meals. Capped at 1,800 characters; a week that doesn't fit becomes today and tomorrow, and the Send sheet says so. A link can't be revoked; the first time, the sender is told who can read it.
- **The read-only view** replaces the app (no ticks, no editing, no onboarding), disables `save()` and blocks `localStorage.setItem` for the page, and adds `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">`. A damaged link says "This link looks incomplete. Ask for it to be sent again."
